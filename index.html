RESEARCH

Exploring how intelligent systems understand information.

My current postgraduate research focuses on Bangla natural language processing, synthetic data generation, and fake-news detection.

MSc Thesis · Natural Language Processing

CLEAN

Classifying Lies and Evaluating Authentic News Using GAN-Augmented Bangla Fake News Detection

CLEAN investigates whether synthetically generated Bangla news can improve fake-news detection. The research combines GAN-based text generation with traditional machine-learning and transformer-based classifiers to study both classification performance and the quality of generated content.

The work uses the BanFakeNews 2.0 dataset and evaluates models across multiple experimental settings, including SeqGAN and LeakGAN augmentation, TF-IDF with LinearSVC, and multilingual and Bangla-specific transformer architectures.
